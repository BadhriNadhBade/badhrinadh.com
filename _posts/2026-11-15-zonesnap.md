title: made zonesnap, a window snapper for macOS
---

Built a small menu-bar app for macOS called ZoneSnap. Press `Ctrl + Option` with the arrow keys, or drag a window to a screen edge, and it snaps into halves, thirds, quarters and more. It has separate top, middle and bottom zones for rotated monitors, and when you resize one window the one next to it fills the gap.

It's free, open source and tested on macOS 12, so if you try it on something newer, let me know how it goes.

More on the [zonesnap page](https://apps.badhrinadh.com/zonesnap/), and the code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/BadhriNadhBade/ZoneSnap).
